Returns and refunds

Try it on
Check the size chart before you order. When the parcel arrives, open it and try the jacket on while the courier waits. If the size does not fit, hand it back to the courier on the spot and we send you the right size.
2 days
You can ask for a refund or an exchange within 2 days of receiving your order. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number.
Condition
The jacket must be unused, in its original condition and in its original packaging.
Timing
Once we approve your request, the refund or exchange is done within 5 business days.
Exchanges
You can change the size or the colour of the jacket. Exchanges for other products are not possible.
Fees
Delivery fees are not refunded. A 75 EGP handling fee is taken off a refund, or charged for an exchange.

Shipping

Where
All 27 governorates in Egypt. We do not ship outside Egypt.
Fee
90 EGP per order, to any governorate. You see it at checkout before you order.
Timing
Orders ship within 4 to 5 business days after we confirm them on WhatsApp.
Payment
Cash on delivery: open the parcel with the courier, then pay. Or pay ahead with InstaPay and send us the screenshot.
Problems
Something wrong with your order? Message us on WhatsApp and we sort it out.
Refunds
Delivery fees are not refunded and cannot be turned into store credit.

Privacy

What we keep
When you order: your name, phone number, governorate, address, the jackets you chose and, if you pay with InstaPay, your payment screenshot. When you create an account: your name, phone number and password. The password is stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, us included.
In your browser
Your cart and language are saved in your browser. A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. To stop people from spamming orders or guessing passwords, we keep a short-lived scrambled record of the internet address each request comes from.
Why
To deliver your order, confirm it with you on WhatsApp, apply your discount code and stop abuse. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics, and we never sell your information.
Who sees it
Our team, and the courier, who gets your name, phone number and address to deliver the parcel. If you send your order on WhatsApp, WhatsApp handles that message. The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, which sees your internet address.
How long
We keep order records for our accounts. You can ask us on WhatsApp to delete your account or your details, and we will, except what the law requires us to keep.
Changes
If we change this page, the new version applies from the day it is posted here. Last updated 27 October 2026.
